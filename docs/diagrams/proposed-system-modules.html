Figure 3.5: Functional Modules of the Proposed System

Yobe State E-Transport Booking and Management System

Passenger Module

  • User registration and login
  • Route and schedule search
  • Interactive seat map selection
  • Online checkout and payment
  • E-ticket with QR code
  • Booking history and cancellation

Administration Module

  • Secure admin authentication
  • Company and bus management
  • Route and schedule management
  • Driver management
  • Booking oversight
  • Ticket verification

Payment & Notification Module

  • Paystack / mock payment processing
  • Payment verification webhooks
  • Email ticket delivery
  • SMS booking notifications
  • Payment status tracking

Reporting & Support Module

  • Operational reports and charts
  • Admin insights dashboard
  • Route map and directions
  • AI support chat assistance
  • Audit and activity logging

Modular decomposition of the proposed system into passenger, admin, payment, and reporting components
