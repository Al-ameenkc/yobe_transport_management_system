Figure 3.1: Existing Manual Transport Booking Workflow

Passenger arrives at motor park
Passenger enquires about trip availability at booking counter
Booking clerk checks paper manifest or verbal seat records
Seat available?
No — passenger waits or tries another operator
Passenger pays fare in cash
Clerk writes paper ticket and records name in manifest book
Passenger waits at terminal until departure time
Attendant verifies paper ticket at boarding point
Driver departs; manager reconciles cash records manually
Trip completed

Source: Field observation and analysis of manual transport operations in Yobe State motor parks
